[p001] (body) I suggest focusing your feedback on these five key areas: 1.Performance evaluation: Define clear, clinically meaningful performance metrics, acceptance thresholds, critical-error criteria, and subgroup performance requirements.
[p002] (body) 2. Risk assessment: Consider expanding the two-axis risk framework to include autonomy, detectability, reversibility, human oversight, patient vulnerability, and potential severity of harm.
[p003] (body) 3. Training & retraining: Establish clear requirements for model retraining, including documentation of training-data changes, version control, bias assessment, and performance comparison before and after retraining.
[p004] (body) 4. Postmarket monitoring: Include proactive monitoring of performance drift, clinically significant errors, near misses, clinician overrides, subgroup performance, and changes in real-world data.
[p005] (body) 5. Modification & revalidation: Define risk-based triggers for reassessment following software updates, algorithm revisions, retraining, or third-party foundation-model changes. Re-benchmarking against the original premarket baseline could provide a practical approach.
[p006] (body) Overall recommendation: Adopt a continuous, risk-adaptive TPLC framework linking premarket evaluation → deployment → postmarket monitoring → retraining/modification → revalidation. This would help maintain safety and effectiveness as GenAI devices evolve.
